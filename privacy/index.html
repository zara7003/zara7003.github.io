Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy describes how applications and integrations operated by Sahil Shaikh ("the Applications", "we", "us") handle information. It applies to the Applications published under the sahilshaikh.org domain and to any associated desktop, web, or server-side components.

1. Scope and Consent

The Applications only access third-party account data after you explicitly authorise access through that provider's own consent screen. If you do not grant access, the Application will not retrieve any of your data. You may revoke access at any time through your provider's account settings.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you enable, the Applications may access:

  • Basic account identifiers (name, email address, profile image);
  • Calendar events, when calendar features are explicitly enabled;
  • Email metadata and message content, when mail features are explicitly enabled;
  • Files and documents you explicitly select or place in a folder you authorise.

The Applications do not access data outside the scopes you grant.

3. How Information Is Used

Accessed information is used solely to provide the feature you requested — for example, retrieving a message, reading a calendar entry, or executing an action you asked for. Data is processed locally on your own systems wherever possible.

4. Google API Services — Limited Use Disclosure

When the Applications use information received from Google APIs, that use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features that are prominent in the Application's interface.
  • Google user data is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with prior notice.
  • Google user data is not used or transferred to third parties for serving advertisements.
  • Google user data is not used for any purpose a human does not read, unless we have your affirmative consent, it is necessary for security purposes, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with prior notice.
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. Storage and Security

Credentials and tokens are stored encrypted on infrastructure controlled by us or by you. Access tokens are never shared, published, or embedded in public code. Data is retained only as long as needed to provide the feature in question and is deleted or anonymised when no longer required.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. Information may be disclosed only where legally required or to protect the rights and safety of users.

7. Third-Party Services

The Applications may interact with third-party APIs (for example Google, Microsoft, or email and calendar providers). Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.
